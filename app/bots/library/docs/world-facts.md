## How to call or dial an international phone number (calling internationally)

Dial your country's exit code, then the country code, then the number without
its leading 0. The exit code is 011 from Canada and the US, and 00 from most
other countries; on a mobile phone, + works everywhere. Example: calling a
Paris number 01 23 45 67 89 from Canada: 011 33 1 23 45 67 89, or
+33 1 23 45 67 89. Canada, the US and much of the Caribbean share +1.
