# Unit conversions

## Exact calculations (the calc bot)

For an exact answer with any number, send a command to the calc bot (if this
node has it enabled): calc 2*(3+4) or calc 15% of 80 for arithmetic, and
convert 10 mi to km, convert 72 f to c or convert 30 psi to bar for units.

## Length and distance conversion

1 inch = 2.54 cm. 1 foot = 12 inches = 30.48 cm. 1 yard = 3 feet = 0.9144 m.
1 mile = 1.609 km = 5,280 feet. 1 nautical mile = 1.852 km. 1 metre = 3.281
feet = 39.37 inches. 1 km = 0.621 miles. Quick: miles × 1.6 = km, km × 0.6 =
miles.

## Weight and mass conversion

1 ounce = 28.35 g. 1 pound = 16 ounces = 453.6 g. 1 kg = 2.205 pounds. 1
stone = 14 pounds = 6.35 kg. 1 metric tonne = 1,000 kg = 2,205 pounds. 1 US
ton = 2,000 pounds = 907 kg. Quick: kg × 2.2 = pounds.

## Volume conversion

1 US teaspoon = 4.93 ml; 1 tablespoon = 3 teaspoons = 14.8 ml. 1 US fluid
ounce = 29.6 ml. 1 US cup = 8 fl oz = 237 ml. 1 US pint = 473 ml, 1 US quart
= 946 ml, 1 US gallon = 3.785 litres. 1 imperial (UK) gallon = 4.546 litres,
1 imperial pint = 568 ml. 1 litre = 1.057 US quarts = 33.8 US fl oz.

## Temperature conversion (Celsius, Fahrenheit, Kelvin)

°F = °C × 9/5 + 32. °C = (°F - 32) × 5/9. K = °C + 273.15. Reference points:
-40 °C = -40 °F; 0 °C = 32 °F (water freezes); 10 °C = 50 °F; 20 °C = 68 °F;
25 °C = 77 °F; 30 °C = 86 °F; 37 °C = 98.6 °F (body); 40 °C = 104 °F;
100 °C = 212 °F (water boils at sea level); 180 °C = 356 °F (350 °F oven);
200 °C = 392 °F (400 °F oven). Quick: °C × 2 + 30 is roughly °F.

## Speed conversion

1 km/h = 0.621 mph = 0.278 m/s. 1 mph = 1.609 km/h. 1 knot = 1 nautical mile
per hour = 1.852 km/h = 1.151 mph. 1 m/s = 3.6 km/h. 100 km/h = 62 mph;
50 km/h = 31 mph.

## Pressure conversion (tire pressure, barometer)

1 bar = 100 kPa = 14.5 psi. 1 psi = 6.895 kPa = 0.0689 bar. Common tire
pressures: 30 psi = 207 kPa = 2.07 bar; 32 psi = 221 kPa = 2.2 bar; 35 psi =
241 kPa = 2.4 bar. Standard air pressure at sea level: 1013.25 hPa = 1013.25
mbar = 101.3 kPa = 29.92 inHg = 760 mmHg = 1 atm.

## Area conversion

1 square foot = 0.0929 m². 1 m² = 10.76 square feet. 1 acre = 4,047 m² =
43,560 square feet = 0.405 hectare. 1 hectare = 10,000 m² = 2.471 acres. 1
km² = 100 hectares = 247 acres = 0.386 square miles. 1 square mile = 640
acres = 2.59 km².

## Fuel economy conversion (mpg and L/100km)

Litres per 100 km = 235.2 ÷ US mpg; US mpg = 235.2 ÷ L/100 km. Examples: 5
L/100 km = 47 mpg; 8 L/100 km = 29 mpg; 10 L/100 km = 23.5 mpg; 12 L/100 km =
19.6 mpg. Imperial (UK) mpg = 282.5 ÷ L/100 km.

## Energy and power conversion

1 kWh = 3.6 MJ = 860 kcal. 1 kcal (food Calorie) = 4.184 kJ. 1 horsepower =
746 W. 1 BTU = 1,055 J; 1 BTU per hour = 0.293 W.

## Cooking measures

1 cup = about 240 ml; 1 tablespoon = 15 ml = 3 teaspoons. 1 cup of flour is
about 125 g, sugar 200 g, rice 185 g, butter 227 g (2 sticks). Oven: 350 °F =
180 °C (gas mark 4), 400 °F = 200 °C (gas mark 6), 425 °F = 220 °C (gas
mark 7).

## Data sizes and transfer speeds

1 byte = 8 bits. 1 KB = 1,000 bytes (1 KiB = 1,024). 1 MB = 1,000 KB, 1 GB =
1,000 MB, 1 TB = 1,000 GB. Network speeds are in bits: a 100 Mbit/s link
moves about 12.5 megabytes per second. A MeshCore text message is at most
about 150 bytes.
